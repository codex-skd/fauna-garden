# Fauna Garden

Adds crops that slowly grow baby animals. Plant a seed for an animal on grass, wait, and a baby
animal appears. Seeds exist for any mob with a spawn egg (configurable through tags), with
variants for water (anemonemals), the Nether (animal shrooms) and lava (magnemones).

## Origin

Fauna Garden is a port of **Animal Crops** by KnightMiner (Minecraft 1.18.2, Forge) to
Minecraft 1.21.1. Licensed under the MIT License (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`). Fauna Garden is
unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original author.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.1.2
- Forge 64.1.x (this branch; also available for 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3 on their own branches) / NeoForge (planned)
- Java 25
